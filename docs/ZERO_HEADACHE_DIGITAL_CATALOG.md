## 3. Detailed Product Specifications

### 3.1 Tier 0: B2C DIY Blueprint & Educational Packs

#### Product 0.1: "Der Deutsche Solar-Carport Baugenehmigungs- & Statik-Guide" (Ebook + Checklist Pack)
* **Target Audience:** Homeowners who want to build a solar carport or terrace but are intimidated by local building regulations (LBO), boundary rules, and snow/wind loads.
* **Format:** 45-Page Illustrated PDF + Notion Planning Template + 16-State Regulatory Cheat Sheet.
* **Key Contents:**
  1. **Landesbauordnung (LBO) Navigator:** Exact procedural rules across all 16 German states (Verfahrensfreiheit limits: e.g., Baden-Württemberg vs. NRW vs. Bayern).
  2. **Grenzbebauung & Abstandsflächen:** How to build directly on the property line legally (max. 3m height, max. 9m length per boundary).
  3. **Statik & Schnee-/Windlast Checkliste:** How to read snow load maps (Zone 1 to 3) and ensure your aluminum profiles withstand up to 2.5 kN/m².
  4. **§12 Abs. 3 UStG 0% MwSt Formular-Vorlage:** How to legally claim 0% VAT on all carport components and accessories.
  5. **Muster-Bauantrags-Anschreiben:** Copy-paste letters and checklist for the local building authority (Bauamt).
* **Pricing:** €47 (Single Purchase).
* **Operational Overhead:** Zero. Pure static digital asset.
* **Upsell Hook:** "Get a €100 voucher off your RIAL Energy Modular Carport Kit when ordering within 30 days."

#### Product 0.2: "DIY Solar-Carport & Terrassendach Master-Montagepaket" (Video Guide + Blueprint Schematics)
* **Target Audience:** DIY enthusiasts, handy homeowners, and independent carpenters/electricians.
* **Format:** HD Video Assembly Modules + Detailed CAD/DXF Assembly Blueprints + Cable Sizing Calculator (Excel/Google Sheets).
* **Key Contents:**

  4. **Elektro-Vorbereitung:** String cable routing, DC surge protection (GAK), inverter positioning, and AC connection preparation for the certified electrician.
* **Pricing:** €97 (Single Purchase).
* **Operational Overhead:** Zero. Hosted on pre-built video portal (e.g. Gumroad/Loom/Vimeo/Shopify Digital).
